A Legacy of Trust, Technology & Patient Comfort Since 1994

Established in 1994, M C Dental Clinic in PJ Extension, Davangere is a trusted one-stop destination for comprehensive oral healthcare. Under the leadership of experienced dental specialists, our practice prioritizes patient satisfaction, hygiene, and cutting-edge treatments.
